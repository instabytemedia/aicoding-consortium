# AICC Royalty-Free Patent Policy

Version 1.0 — normative for all consortium work. Modeled on the W3C Patent Policy (RF mode).

1. **RF commitment.** Every working-group participant commits, as a condition of participation,
   to grant a royalty-free, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive license to all patent claims
   essential to implementing any AICC specification the participant's working group publishes.
2. **Scope.** The commitment covers claims essential to Recommendation-track text the
   participant contributed to or reviewed while a WG member, and survives leaving the WG.
3. **Disclosure.** Participants must disclose known essential claims during the review period of
   every maturity transition (Editor's Draft → Working Draft → Candidate Rec. → Recommendation).
4. **Undisclosed claims.** Essential claims a participant fails to disclose are automatically
   licensed under §1.
5. **Exclusion.** A participant may exclude specific claims within 45 days of a Working Draft's
   publication by written notice; exclusion terminates the participant's WG membership.
6. **Defensive suspension.** A license under §1 may be suspended against any party that asserts
   an essential-claim infringement suit regarding the same specification.
7. **Non-participants.** Specification text is CC-BY-4.0; this policy binds participants.
   Implementers who are not participants receive the benefit of every §1 grant.

Questions: governance@aicodingconsortium.org (after domain setup) or the public issue tracker.
